# ReelStage – App Store listing draft

**App name:** ReelStage – Video Reels Carousel
**Tagline (max 62 chars):** Show your Instagram reels in a centre-stage video carousel

## Description
Turn your Instagram reels into a beautiful, auto-playing video carousel on your store.

- Connect your Instagram Business or Creator account in one click
- Pick the reels you want and put them in any order
- The playing reel sits in the centre and moves to the next one when it ends, in a loop
- Videos are stored in your own Shopify Files, so they load fast and never break
- Add the block to any page with the theme editor; no code needed
- Change sizes, colours, corners, caption and button to match your brand
- Works on phones with swipe, sound on/off and pause

## Key benefits (3)
1. **Set up in two minutes**: connect, pick, add the block.
2. **Fast and reliable**: reels play from Shopify's own CDN.
3. **Made for every theme**: works with all Online Store 2.0 themes.
